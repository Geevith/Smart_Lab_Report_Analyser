FAQ

Is my data secure?

Yes. File analysis is performed with strict privacy controls. In this demo/beta version, data is processed in ephemeral sessions and is not stored permanently.

What happens if a test isn't recognized?

The system flags unrecognized parameters. You can view these in the "Validation Summary" step. Always compare results with your original PDF.

Can I analyze handwritten reports?

Our OCR is optimized for digital text. Handwritten reports may have lower accuracy and we recommend using printed lab reports for best results.
