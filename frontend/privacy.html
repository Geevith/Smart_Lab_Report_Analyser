Smart Lab Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026  ·  Your privacy matters to us

🔒 Our commitment: We are committed to protecting your health data. We do not sell your personal information to third parties, ever.

1. Who We Are

Smart Lab Report Analyser ("we", "us", or "our") is a health intelligence platform that enables users to upload and analyse medical lab reports using AI. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

Category Data Collected Purpose
Account Info Username, email, password (hashed), full name, gender, date of birth, age, ethnicity (optional) Account creation, personalised analysis, age/gender-specific reference ranges
Health Reports Uploaded files, extracted text, analysis results, parameters Core analysis service, historical tracking
Usage Data Login timestamps, actions performed, IP address, browser type Security monitoring, audit trail, service improvement
Session Data Session tokens (stored as HTTP-only cookies) Authentication and session management

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Smart Lab service
  • Generate personalised lab report analysis using your age, gender, and ethnicity where relevant to reference ranges
  • Maintain your report history and enable longitudinal health tracking
  • Send important security and account notifications
  • Detect and prevent fraud and unauthorised activity
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your health data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purposes unrelated to the service.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted infrastructure partners (e.g., database hosting via Supabase/PostgreSQL, deployment via Render) under strict data processing agreements
  • AI Models: If you upload a narrative report (CT, MRI, etc.), the extracted text may be sent to the Google Gemini API for analysis. No personally identifiable information is transmitted as part of the analysis prompt
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or regulatory authority

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — we never store plain-text passwords
  • Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies with CSRF protection
  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Database access is restricted and monitored
  • Uploaded files are processed temporarily and deleted after analysis

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

🔍 Access Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
✏️ Correction Update inaccurate or incomplete personal data
🗑️ Deletion Request deletion of your account and associated data
📦 Portability Export your report data in standard formats (CSV, Excel)
🚫 Objection Object to or restrict certain processing of your data
↩️ Withdraw Consent Withdraw your consent for data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@smartlab.app.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data and reports for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will permanently delete your personal data and health reports within 30 days. Anonymised, aggregated usage statistics may be retained for service improvement.

8. Cookies

We use only essential cookies necessary for the operation of the Service:

  • session_token – An HTTP-only, secure cookie used for authentication
  • csrf_token – A CSRF protection token to prevent cross-site request forgery

We do not use tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that a minor has registered, we will promptly delete their account.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will provide notice within the application or via email. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

📬 Data Protection Enquiries

For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your data rights, please contact us at support@smartlab.app. We aim to respond within 30 days.
